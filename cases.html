Organized Retail Theft Ring Dismantled

📍 Downtown London 📅 January 2026 👥 4 Officers Deployed

Multi-location investigation identified a coordinated group targeting high-value electronics. Through coordinated surveillance and intelligence gathering, our team tracked patterns across 5 retail locations over 3 weeks.

Actions Taken: Coordinated apprehension operation with local police, executed simultaneous detentions, recovered stolen merchandise, and provided comprehensive evidence packages.

✓ $420 CAD merchandise recovered • 3 apprehensions • Zero incidents

Internal Shrinkage Investigation

📍 North London Mall 📅 December 2025 🔍 CCTV Analysis

Client reported consistent inventory discrepancies in the cosmetics department. Our loss prevention specialists conducted a thorough review of CCTV footage, POS data, and employee access patterns.

Actions Taken: Identified procedural vulnerabilities, implemented new checkout verification protocols, and provided staff retraining on proper merchandise handling.

📊 40% reduction in shrinkage • New protocols implemented

Shoplifting Deterrence Initiative

📍 Westmount Plaza 📅 November 2025 👮 Visible Patrols

High-traffic retail location experiencing increased shoplifting incidents during holiday season. Implemented enhanced visible security presence combined with strategic positioning and customer engagement protocols.

Actions Taken: Increased officer visibility, established entry/exit monitoring, coordinated with store management on high-risk item placement, and conducted daily briefings.

✓ 67% reduction in incidents • $310 CAD recovered • Enhanced customer safety

Return Fraud Prevention

📍 South London 📅 October 2025 📋 Policy Review

Client identified suspicious return patterns totaling significant losses. Our team analyzed return transaction data, identified fraudulent patterns, and developed targeted prevention strategies.
